Libretto Finder

The best way into an opera is to listen while reading the original and a translation side by side. Finding those pages means searching, and searching, and searching. This is the list, so you don't have to.

Single-language pages are listed too, marked original or translation: useful, but not the thing. Every link has an archived copy, because libretto sites die.

Sources: LibrettoArchive and the late rwagner.net (side by side, English and others), Kareol (original plus Spanish, side by side, hundreds of operas, indexed here under the original titles), and opera-guide.ch (single-language pages). Not indexed per opera: The Book of 101 Opera Librettos on the Internet Archive (facing-page translations, borrowable), and IMSLP for scanned vocal scores that often carry a singing translation under the original.
