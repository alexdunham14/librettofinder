Libretto Finder

The best way into an opera is to listen while reading the original and a translation side by side. Finding those pages means searching, and searching, and searching. This is the list, so you don't have to.

Links marked translation only are single-language pages: useful, but not the thing. Every link has an archived copy, because libretto sites die.

Other places worth knowing that are not indexed per opera here: Kareol (original plus Spanish, side by side, hundreds of operas), The Book of 101 Opera Librettos on the Internet Archive (facing-page translations, borrowable), and IMSLP for scanned vocal scores that often carry a singing translation under the original.
